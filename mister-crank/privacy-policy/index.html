Mister Crank — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what Mister Crank, made by Perfect Joust, collects when you play, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • An install report each time the game starts: the app's ID and version, the device type (Fire TV or Fire tablet), when the game was installed and last updated, and which other Perfect Joust games are installed on the same device.
  • With your consent to gameplay analytics: app activity (sessions, levels played and finished, Daily Challenges, Hunter rounds, playtime) and in-app purchase history (item and price).
  • If you also allow "Count me as one player": your device's advertising ID, so repeat visits count as one player.
  • If you allow gameplay analytics but not "Count me as one player": a random ID created for this game on this device instead of the advertising ID.

What we do not collect

Your name, email address, location, contacts, or anything you type.

Why we collect it

To understand how the game is played and to fix and balance levels, and to see which Perfect Joust games are on a device. We do not show ads in the game, we do not sell your data, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who we share it with

RevMetrik (analytics.revmetrik.com), Perfect Joust's own analytics service, which processes the data for us. Payments are handled by Amazon; we never see your payment details.

Your consent

On first launch the game asks before any gameplay analytics are sent. "Accept all" turns on gameplay analytics and "Count me as one player" (the advertising ID); under "Your choices" you can turn either off.

Your choices

You can change your choices at any time in the game's Settings. You can also reset your advertising ID in your Fire device's settings.

Storage

Your game progress is saved on your device.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Amazon. We never see your payment details.

Advertising

Mister Crank shows no advertising.

Children

Mister Crank is a game for all ages and is not directed at children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child's information was collected can contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: perfectgamesjoust1@outlook.com
