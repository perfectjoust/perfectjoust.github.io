Mister Crank — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what Mister Crank, made by Perfect Joust, collects when you play, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

App activity (sessions, levels, Daily Challenges, Hunter rounds, playtime) and in-app purchase history (item and price), plus game version and device type.

What we do not collect

Name, email, location, contacts, advertising ID or any other device identifier, anything typed.

Why we collect it

Analytics only, to fix and balance levels. Not used for ads or tracking, not sold, not linked to identity.

Who we share it with

RevMetrik (analytics.revmetrik.com), the developer's own analytics service. Payments are handled by Amazon.

Your consent

Asked on the first-launch panel; nothing is collected before the player confirms.

Your choices

Players can turn Game stats off any time in Settings.

Storage

Progress is saved on the device.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Amazon. We never see your payment details.

Advertising

Mister Crank shows no advertising.

Children

Mister Crank is made for players of all ages, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child's information was collected can contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new date.
