Mister Crank — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Mister Crank is made by Perfect Joust. Here is what the game sends us, what we do with it, and how you stay in control. The same rules apply to our other games. Our website keeps no data and sets no cookies.

Nothing leaves your device until you say yes

When you first start the game, a "Before you play" screen asks for your permission. "OK, let's play!" turns on both "Ad results" and "Play stats". Tap "Manage options" to switch either one off. You can change this later in Settings, under "Privacy choices".

What the game sends

  • Ad results: your device's advertising ID and Android ID, and which of our game ads you saw or opened. If you switch this off, the game uses a random number made only for this game on this device instead.
  • Play stats: when you play, for how long, and which levels you start, finish or lose.
  • Device basics: device model, Android version, language, Amazon marketplace, and which Perfect Joust games are installed.
  • Purchases: what you bought, its price and Amazon's receipt number, so we can count sales.

The game never asks for or sends your name, email, phone number, contacts, exact location, photos or files.

What we use it for

We use it to see how the game is played and fix what is not working, and to promote our own games: showing ads for them, offering things like Mister Crank's VIP, not repeating the same ad, and not advertising games you already have. That is all.

Who else sees it

Nobody. The data goes to servers that Perfect Joust runs, kept by a hosting provider who stores it for us and cannot use it. We never sell it, and we do not work with ad networks, data brokers or outside analytics companies. We would only hand data over if a court or the law required it.

Buying things

Purchases go through Amazon. Amazon handles your payment; we never see your card or payment details.

Your progress

Your levels, coins and unlocks are saved on your own device.

Turning things off

  • Switch "Ad results" or "Play stats" off in Settings > Privacy choices.
  • Reset your advertising ID: on Fire TV in Settings > Preferences > Privacy Settings; on Fire tablets in Settings > Privacy > Advertising ID.
  • Email us to have your data removed. We may ask for your advertising ID to find it.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it for the uses above. After that it is deleted or made anonymous, or earlier if you ask. It travels over encrypted connections, and only the people who need it can open it.

Your rights

Depending on your country, you can ask to see, copy, correct or delete your data, or to limit how we use it. In the EEA and the UK we rely on your permission, which you can take back at any time; California residents have similar rights. Just email us. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Kids

Mister Crank is made for everyone and is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without consent. Parents can email us and we will delete their child's data.

Updates

When this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date. If we start collecting something new, the game will ask for your permission again.
