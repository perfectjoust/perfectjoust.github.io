Mister Crank — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy covers Mister Crank and the other games made by Perfect Joust, and this website.

1. The short version

With your consent, our games collect your device ID and advertising ID, and how you play. This goes only to servers we run. We use it to understand how our games are played and to show you ads for our own games. We never sell it or share it with advertisers. This website collects nothing and uses no cookies.

2. Information we collect

With your consent, our games collect:

  • your device ID (Android ID) and your device's advertising ID;
  • basic device details: the device model, Android version, language and Amazon marketplace, and which of our games are installed;
  • how you play, linked to those IDs: which of our games you play and for how long, the levels you start, win or lose, and which of our ads you were shown or tapped;
  • what you bought in the game (the item, its price and the store's receipt number), so we can count sales.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos or files. Purchases are made through the app store you downloaded the game from; the store keeps your payment details and we never see them.

3. Your consent

The first time you open one of our games, it asks for your consent ("Before you play"). "OK, let's play!" allows both "Ad results" and "Play stats"; "Manage options" lets you turn either off. With "Ad results" off, the game sends a random ID made for that game on that device instead of your device ID and advertising ID. With "Play stats" off, it sends no play data. You can change your mind at any time.

4. How we use this information

  • Analytics: to understand how our games are played, fix problems and decide what to make next.
  • Advertising our own games: to show you ads for our other games and offers in our games (such as Mister Crank's VIP), avoid showing the same ad too often or ads for games you already have, and see which ads work.

We do not use third-party advertisers or ad networks, and we do not use this data for anything else.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your data, and we do not share it with advertisers, ad networks, data brokers or analytics companies. It goes only to servers we operate. The company that hosts those servers stores the data on our behalf and may not use it for its own purposes. We disclose data only if the law requires it, for example under a valid court order.

6. Your choices

  • Turn "Ad results" or "Play stats" off at any time in the game's Settings, under "Privacy choices", or email us.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of interest-based ads. On Amazon Fire TV: Settings > Preferences > Privacy Settings. On Fire tablets: Settings > Privacy > Advertising ID. On other Android devices: Settings > Privacy > Ads. We respect your choice to opt out of interest-based ads.
  • Ask us to delete your data by email. We may ask for your advertising ID so we can find it.

7. How long we keep it, and how we protect it

We keep this data only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, then delete or anonymize it, or sooner if you ask us to delete it. It is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and only people who need it can access it.

8. Children

Our games are for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the age of parental consent where you live) without consent. A parent or guardian can email us to have a child's data deleted.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, get a copy of, correct or delete your data, to restrict its use, or to object to it. If you are in the EEA or the UK, we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. California residents have similar rights. To use any of these rights, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. If we change what we collect or how we use it, our games will ask for your consent again.
