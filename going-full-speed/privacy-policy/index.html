What we collect What we don't Why Where it goes Your consent Your choices Purchases Children

1What we collect

  • AlwaysBasic device information each time the game starts: the game's ID and version, the device model and maker, the Android version, the device language and region, when the game was installed, and which other Perfect Joust games are installed on the same device.
  • AlwaysWhich of our own game promotions you saw, opened or installed.
  • With "Ad results"Your device's advertising ID and Android ID. With "Ad results" off, a random ID made for this game on this device is sent instead.
  • With "Play stats"How long and how often you play, and which races you start, win or lose.
  • VIP offerWhen you see our VIP offer: which offer and price you saw and whether you bought it, so we can see which offer works best.

2What we do not collect

Your name, email address, precise location, contacts, or anything you type.

3Why we collect it

To suggest other Perfect Joust games you might like, to avoid showing you games you already have, to see which of our promotions work, and to understand how the game is played so we can fix and balance it.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4Where it goes

The data is sent only to Perfect Joust's own cross-promotion and analytics service. We do not share it with any other company. Purchases are handled by Amazon; we never see your payment details.

5Your consent

On first launch the game asks before any device IDs are sent. "Allow all" turns on both "Ad results" and "Play stats"; under "Manage options" you can turn either off.

6Your choices

You can reset or limit your advertising ID at any time in your Fire device's settings. To change your in-game choices, clear the game's data or reinstall it and choose again on the first screen.

7Storage

Your game progress, coins and balls are saved on your device.

8Purchases

Coin packs and VIP are bought through Amazon's In-App Purchasing. Amazon processes the payment; we never see your payment details.

9Advertising

Going Full Speed shows promotions for other Perfect Joust games only (for example the "More Games" screen). It shows no advertising from other companies.

10Children

Going Full Speed is a game for all ages and is not directed at children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child's information was collected can contact us and we will delete it.

11Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new date.
